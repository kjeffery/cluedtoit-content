Clued To It — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

Clued To It is a party guessing game for iOS. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you.

Information we collect

None. Clued To It does not collect, log, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, and no advertising SDKs.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device only. It is never uploaded anywhere:

  • Your game settings (round mode, timer duration, tilt vs. buttons, deck update preference).
  • Which category cards you've seen, so the app can show you fresh content.
  • Whether you've purchased the Unlock Everything in-app purchase.

Deleting the app removes this data.

Network usage

The app makes network requests to kjeffery.github.io/cluedtoit-content to check for and download updated card content. These requests:

  • Are anonymous — no device identifier, account, or personal data is attached.
  • Retrieve only card content (JSON files). The app never downloads executable code.
  • Are logged by GitHub Pages as standard web server access logs (IP address, User-Agent). We do not have access to these logs and do not analyze them. See GitHub's privacy statement.

You can disable automatic update checks in Settings → Deck Updates → Never. The app will continue to work with the content that shipped in the app.

In-app purchases

The "Unlock Everything" in-app purchase is processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We do not see or store your payment information. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles purchase data.

Children

Clued To It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new policy will replace this page.
